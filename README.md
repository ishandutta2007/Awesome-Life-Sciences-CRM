# Awesome-Life-Sciences-CRM

## Top Life Sciences CRM Tools Ecosystem

**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**  
*Focused on Pharmaceutical Field Engagement, Clinical Trial Support & Healthcare Provider Relationships*  
**Last updated: March 2026**

This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Life Sciences CRM Tools**. These tools help pharmaceutical, biotech, and medical device companies manage healthcare provider relationships, track field interactions, support clinical trials, and ensure regulatory compliance — often with specialized features for medical inquiries, sample management, and multichannel engagement.

**Examples** include Salesforce Life Sciences Cloud, Veeva CRM, IQVIA Orchestrated Customer Engagement, Model N, Pitcher Life Sciences, Exeevo, Aktana, Trueblue, WhizAI, and Platforce (the category leaders).

**Open-source emphasis**: This section is heavily expanded with every major active project for self-hosting, custom life sciences workflows, and transparent clinical data management — ideal for research institutions, small biotech firms, and developers building data-sovereign healthcare CRM platforms.

Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms

- **[Salesforce Life Sciences Cloud](https://www.salesforce.com/)**  
  Salesforce's purpose-built life sciences CRM entering the market with native Einstein AI, integration with Health Cloud and Data Cloud, and partnership with IQVIA to leverage OCE technology [citation:18][citation:19].

- **[Veeva CRM](https://www.veeva.com/)**  
  Industry-leading life sciences CRM with deep pharmaceutical field engagement features, medical inquiry management, and sample tracking. Transitioning from Salesforce platform to Veeva Vault [citation:1][citation:18].

- **[IQVIA Orchestrated Customer Engagement (OCE)](https://www.iqvia.com/)**  
  Enterprise CRM for life sciences with advanced analytics, real-world data integration, and omnichannel orchestration. Rated 4.8 stars on Gartner Peer Insights [citation:2][citation:19].

- **[Model N](https://www.modeln.com/)**  
  Revenue management and CRM platform for life sciences with pricing, contracting, and channel data management.

- **[Pitcher Life Sciences](https://www.pitcher.com/)**  
  Sales enablement and CRM platform for life sciences with content management, closed-loop marketing, and field engagement tools [citation:1][citation:7].

- **[Exeevo](https://www.exeevo.com/)**  
  Omnichannel CRM for life sciences with AI-driven engagement and field force effectiveness tools.

- **[Aktana](https://www.aktana.com/)**  
  AI-powered decision support and next-best-action platform for life sciences commercial teams.

- **[Trueblue](https://www.trueblue.com/)**  
  Life sciences CRM with deep domain expertise, pragmatic implementation approach, and focus on real-world value delivery [citation:1][citation:7].

- **[WhizAI](https://www.whiz.ai/)**  
  AI-powered analytics platform for life sciences commercial operations with natural language querying.

- **[Platforce](https://platforce.com/)**  
  Salesforce-native CRM solution for life sciences with field engagement, medical inquiries, and compliance features.

## Open-Source GitHub Projects

- **[clinic EDC](https://github.com/clinicedc/clinicedc)**  
  Open-source clinical trials data management framework built on Django for multisite randomized longitudinal clinical trials. Provides modules for EDC/eSource systems handling data collection across HIV/AIDS, diabetes, hypertension, and integrated care research. Used by Harvard T.H. Chan School of Public Health, Botswana-Harvard AIDS Institute, and London School of Hygiene & Tropical Medicine. Python 3.12+, Django 5.2+, MySQL 8+ [citation:5][citation:16].

- **[LibreClinica](https://github.com/reliatec-gmbh/LibreClinica)**  
  Community-driven open-source successor to OpenClinica for Electronic Data Capture (EDC) and Clinical Data Management (CDM). Forked from OpenClinica 3.14 in 2019. Supports PostgreSQL 13/14 with Tomcat 9 and OpenJDK 11. Used by university hospitals and research institutions [citation:10].

- **[CRMSalud](https://github.com/danjohn007/CRMSalud)**  
  Open-source CRM specialized for the health sector with client management, product catalog, sales pipeline, and analytics. Built with PHP/MySQL, deployable on XAMPP/WAMP or LAMP. Role-based access for administrators, sales, marketing, and inventory [citation:4].

- **[PharmaCRM (crm-contact-and-activity-tracker-ui)](https://gitlab.com/hostdesign24/crm-contact-and-activity-tracker-ui)**  
  Specialized CRM building block for pharmaceutical contact management and interaction tracking. Spring Boot backend with React frontend. Features contact CRUD with soft-delete, activity logging (CALL, EMAIL, MEETING) with timestamps and notes, contact-specific summaries, and interactive dashboard with network growth metrics [citation:9].

- **[restructure](https://github.com/consected/restructure)**  
  Open-source platform for structuring, tracking, recruiting, screening, navigating, and reviewing human subjects research studies. Custom reactive JavaScript frontend with API authentication. Used by Harvard Medical School Football Players Health Study, Harvard Pilgrim Health Care Institute Project Viva. BSD-3 licensed, copyright Harvard University [citation:3].

- **[ERPNext](https://github.com/frappe/erpnext)**  
  Open-source ERP with CRM, manufacturing, inventory, and healthcare modules adaptable for life sciences operations. Used by ANSAL Integrated Pharma Solutions for managing pharmaceutical consulting clients, pharmacy partners, and pharmacist candidate pipelines [citation:15].

- **[openCRX](https://github.com/openCRX/openCRX)**  
  Open-source enterprise CRM with sales force automation, opportunity management, and workflow capabilities. Listed as suitable for pharmaceutical, cosmetics, and chemical industries. Deployed by companies managing production, quality control, and export operations with ERP integration [citation:14].

### Additional Strong Open-Source Options

- **[Lab Manager](https://github.com/)** — Lightweight LIMS for clinical molecular laboratories (Node.js/Express, GPL) organizing sequencing results and lab workflows.
- **[iSkyLIMS](https://github.com/)** — GPL-licensed LIMS for genomic facilities focusing on massive sequencing workflows.
- **[NEMO](https://github.com/)** — MIT-licensed laboratory logistics and resource management system from NIST for equipment scheduling and maintenance tracking.
- **[Sequencescape](https://github.com/)** — Scalable open LIMS from Wellcome Sanger Institute for high-throughput sequencing workflows.
- **Clinical Trial Tools** — OpenClinica, LibreClinica, and clinicedc for EDC and clinical data management.
- **Laboratory Management** — Bika/Senaite LIMS, MISO for NGS labs, and gP2S for cryo-EM facilities.

**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Combine **clinic EDC** or **LibreClinica** for clinical trial data, **PharmaCRM** for field engagement, **ERPNext** for ERP and CRM integration, and **openCRX** for enterprise sales management to build a complete self-hosted life sciences CRM stack.
